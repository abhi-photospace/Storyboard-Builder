About the Developer

Hi, I'm Abhinandan R Bharadwaj (Abhi), a film student at Srishti Manipal Institute of Art, Design and Technology in Bengaluru, and an aspiring filmmaker. I also freelance under Abhi Photospace, shooting and editing visual stories.

Why I built this

During our odd semester summative assessment, it was pre-production day and I had just finished drawing my storyboard. Every time before, I'd import photos of my drawings or location shots into Photoshop with the template our faculty provided, then align everything by hand and add layer after layer of text for each field. It was a hassle.

I thought: what if an app could just do this? Import your images, type your text, done. I found a few websites that did something similar, but they were paid, the free versions were limited, and none had the exact template or features I wanted.

So I made my own, vibe coded from scratch. Once the basics worked, I kept adding tools to help visualise frames: sketching and annotation, a presentation mode, shot-list export, and more.

Beyond the screen

This app is built by a filmmaker, for filmmakers.
If it saves you even a few minutes of Photoshop pain, it did its job.
